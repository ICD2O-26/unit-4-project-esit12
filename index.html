Ray Tracing

A Modern Rendering Technology

What is this technology?

Ray tracing is a rendering technique used to simulate realistic lighting in digital images. It calculates how light interacts with objects to produce highly detailed and photo-realistic visuals.

What problem does this technology solve?

Ray tracing improves rendering accuracy by simulating real-world light behavior such as reflection, refraction, shadows, and global illumination more realistically than traditional rasterization.

How does it work?

Ray tracing works by tracing rays from the camera into a scene and calculating how they interact with surfaces, materials, and light sources to generate realistic images.

Where is it used?

Ray tracing is widely used in video games, animation, film production, 3D modeling, and simulation technologies.

History

The idea of ray tracing dates back centuries in optics and geometry, but modern computational ray tracing was developed in the 1960s and became widely practical in graphics with GPU acceleration technologies like NVIDIA RTX in 2018.
